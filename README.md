Entry for the make.fun bounty "Ridiculous at First Glance, Real by the Second": a single sentence pitching a bizarre startup that turns out to be a real business. The pitch: bottled genuine new-car smell, sold by the litre to used-car dealers, whose per-car spray cost is repaid many times over in a higher sale price — so they reorder monthly. The program validates the sentence against the bounty's caps (one sentence, at most 200 characters) and writes it to entry.txt.
